ACT 3: The Accountability Trail

The House has heard the nominees. The committee has reported. One question decides 19 cabinet appointments — and on 7 August 2024, the question was put and agreed to in under a minute. This page asks the only question that matters afterwards: who, exactly, approved?

House
National Assembly

Committee on Appointments — Second Report
Outcome
19 Cabinet Secretary nominees approved · 1 rejected: Ms Stella Soi Lang'at
The Finding

All 19 approvals passed by voice vote. No per-MP recorded vote exists — anywhere.

Hansard records exactly one line for the decision of the whole House: “(Question put and agreed to)”. No division was called, no member's vote was recorded, no aye/no tally exists. Every MP's vote on every nominee is, officially, unknown. The blankness below is not missing data — it is the public record.

How to read this: the empty cells are the accountability finding. When the House approves cabinet nominees by voice vote, no MP — for any nominee — is on record. Ask any member on this list how they voted on any of the 19: the honest answer available to the public is “there is no record”.

Contrast — a vote that exists

Finance Bill 2024, Second Reading — a recorded division

When the House does take a recorded division, every member's name is on the roll. The Finance Bill 2024 Second Reading produced exactly that: 192 ayes, 105 noes, each attributed on Mzalendo's voting record. The mechanism exists. It was simply never used for the 19 cabinet approvals.

The one rejection that WAS individually recorded

The House never named who voted for the 19 — but it named, and reasoned on the record, the one nominee it voted down.

Rejected · 7 Aug 2024

Ms Stella Soi Lang'at

Cabinet Secretary nominee — Gender, Culture, Arts & Heritage

House decision: NOT APPROVED — 19 of 20 nominees carried; Lang'at alone rejected

“Failed to demonstrate adequate knowledge of topical, administrative and technical issues touching on the portfolio.”

— Committee on Appointments, Second Report on the Vetting of Cabinet Secretary Nominees, August 2024

“Her rejection is not in any way an indictment of her capability or competence to perform other responsibilities.”

— Hon. Peter Obokato, Hansard, National Assembly afternoon sitting, 7 August 2024

The asymmetry: one nominee's rejection earned reasons, debate, and a named MP's argument on the Hansard record. Nineteen approvals earned a single line — “(Question put and agreed to)” — and no voter's name at all.
