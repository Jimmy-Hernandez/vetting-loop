# Attribution and source terms

The record is compiled from public documents. Each item in the app carries its own
source link; this file records the terms attached to the underlying collections.

## Mzalendo — voting records

MP voting histories, division results and scorecards used in this repo are published by
**Mzalendo Trust** (mzalendo.com) under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

- Division CSVs and samples: `data/mzalendo/`
- Attribution: Mzalendo Trust — https://mzalendo.com
- ShareAlike: derivative datasets built from this material must carry the same license.

## Parliament of Kenya — Hansard and committee reports

- Hansard transcripts and the Committee on Appointments' own reports are public
  documents of the Parliament of Kenya.
- OCR output of the 2024 committee report lives in `data/parliament-pdf/`.
- Line references in the compiled record point into that OCR text so any claim can be
  checked against the page it came from.

## News publishers

Individual integrity flags cite the publisher and date on the flag itself. Those items
remain the copyright of the publishers named; they are quoted here for the purpose of
documenting what was reported, with a link back to the original.

Items carrying `needs_verification` have not yet been checked against a primary source.
