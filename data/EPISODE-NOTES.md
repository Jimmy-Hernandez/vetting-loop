# Episode Selection: August 2024 CS Vetting (THE MVP EPISODE)

Date selected: 2026-09-24 (hackathon). All claims below have a verified source.

## The episode
15–23 July 2024: President Ruto nominates 21 (20 vetted for CS + re-shuffle context).
1–4 Aug 2024: Committee on Appointments (chaired by Speaker Wetang'ula, 20 members incl. Deputy Speaker Gladys Boss) holds public approval hearings at County Hall, 7am–10:30pm days.
Public submissions: 1,300+ received (Wetang'ula statement); 163 affidavits contested 10 nominees over prior dismissals; 187 submissions were s.6(9) Act affidavits.
5 Aug: committee retreats to write report.
7 Aug 2024 (Wed afternoon sitting): Committee presents Second Report. House approves 19 CS nominees, REJECTS Stella Soi Lang'at (Gender/Culture/Arts & Heritage) — "failed to demonstrate adequate knowledge of her docket"; frequent job transitions noted (Report p.238, observation 8).
**VOTE MECHANISM: "(Question put and agreed to)" — voice vote. NO per-MP recorded division exists.**

## Why it's the signature demo
- Integrity-flagged-but-approved nominees: Hassan Joho (qualifications/conflict of interest), Wycliffe Oparanya (EACC corruption allegations — "never called to record a statement", kids' phones seized at Integrity Centre), Alfred Mutua (Judy Chepchirchir labour-agent scandal; prior public pronouncements).
- The committee DID ask some questions — so the demo is nuanced: ASKED (with source) vs flags from memoranda the record doesn't show asked.
- The FINAL VOTE itself: 19 approvals with no recorded vote. The accountability gap is the vote itself — "who approved?" has no answer on any public record. Per-MP query returns: no division recorded.
- Precedent detail: Phyllis Kandie 2013 — committee rejected, House "saved" her (Hansard 7 Aug 2024). Shows House CAN amend.
- Wandayi exclusion: nominee was an Appointments Committee member; couldn't sit for his own vetting (Hansard, Ichung'wah).

## Committee's own integrity-check apparatus (Act 1 rails!)
Committee sought references from: EACC (ethics/integrity), HELB (loan repayment), DCI (criminal records), ORPP (party office), KRA (tax compliance), CUE (certificate authenticity). → Our Nominee File = these checks + memoranda + hearing Q&A, source-linked.
