PARLIAMENT OF KENYA

Wednesday, 7th August, 2024 Morning Sitting

The House met at the Senate Chamber, Parliament Buildings, at 9.30 a.m.

[The Speaker (Hon. Kingi) in the Chair]

PRAYER

DETERMINATION OF QUORUM AT COMMENCEMENT OF SITTING

The Speaker (Hon. Kingi)

Clerk, do we have quorum? Serjeant-at-Arms, kindly ring the Quorum Bell for 10 minutes.

(The Quorum Bell was rung)

Hon. Members, be upstanding.

ADJOURNMENT

Hon. Senators, there being no quorum and having rung the Quorum Bell pursuant to the provisions of Standing Order No.40(2), the Senate stands adjourned until today, Wednesday, 7th August, 2024, at 2.30 p.m.

The Senate rose at 9.45 a.m.

August 07, 2024 SENATE DEBATES 1
