# Integrity-Flag Dossier — August 2024 Kenyan CS Vetting Episode

_Compiled 2026-09-24. Scope: documented, publicly reported integrity-related issues existing BEFORE or DURING the vetting (1–4 Aug 2024, National Assembly Committee on Appointments, chaired by Speaker Moses Wetang'ula; 20 nominees vetted, 19 approved, Stella Soi rejected 7 Aug)._

## Method & integrity rules
- Every flag carries: claim, direct quote, publisher, URL, date, and legal status. Claims are verbatim-reported; no inference or editorial judgement is added.
- Sourcing preference: Daily Nation, The Standard/TNX, Business Daily, Capital FM, Citizen Digital, The Star, parliament.go.ke; Kenyans.co.ke and Nairobi Wire/Eastleigh Voice used where they carry on-the-record quotes; each was checked against a second source where possible.
- ZERO flags is the honest result for a nominee where no credible reporting was found — do not read silence as guilt.

## Cross-episode facts (context)
- Ruto dissolved his entire Cabinet on **11 July 2024** under Gen-Z protest pressure; the Speaker said of the re-nominated survivors: *"The people who can't serve are on account of violation of Chapter Six... These Cabinet Secretaries were simply dismissed... There are no allegations against them. They just went with everybody."* (Capital FM, 22 Jul 2024).
- Parliament's public-participation exercise drew **>1,300 memoranda**; the top objectors recorded were **Murkomen 92, Joho 72, Duale 69, Kindiki 68, Chirchir 58** (Daily Nation, 1 Aug 2024). **Note on the '163 affidavits' lead:** verified reporting documents the >1,300-memoranda total and these per-nominee counts; a specific '163 affidavits vs 10 nominees' figure could NOT be verified in mainstream reporting during this run and is NOT asserted here.
- A parallel KHRC constitutional-petition line reported 813 memoranda of suitability/unsuitability, 656 struck out on procedural grounds (same National Assembly analysis family).
