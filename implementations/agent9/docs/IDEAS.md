# Stretch Goals and Future Ideas

This document outlines 30+ distinct feature ideas organized by category.

## 1. Civic Intelligence
- **Semantic similarity search**: Semantic matching between citizen questions and MP asked questions. [effort: L] [impact: High]
- **Auto-flag generation**: AI ingestion of EACC database to auto-generate draft flags. [effort: L] [impact: High]
- **Hearing sentiment analysis**: Analyzing the tone of the hearing using AI. [effort: M] [impact: Med]
- **Dossier summarization**: LLM summaries of complex legal documents for citizens. [effort: M] [impact: High]

## 2. Transparency Mechanics
- **Personal alert loop**: 'Did your MP ask your question?' personalized email alert. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Constituency vetting score**: Aggregate metric showing how well an MP represented constituency concerns. [effort: M] [impact: Med]
- **Hypocrisy tracker**: Showing if MPs complain about issues they previously rubber-stamped. [effort: L] [impact: Med]
- **Source verified badges**: Clear indicators for flags that have been independently audited. [effort: S] [impact: High]

## 3. Data Integrations
- **EACC live API integration**: Automatically pull in new corruption cases. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Kenya Gazette RSS**: Parse the Gazette for new nomination announcements automatically. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Mzalendo lookup**: Deep link MP profiles to Mzalendo for historical context. [effort: S] [impact: Med]
- **eCitizen court records**: Integration with judiciary for civil/criminal case tracking. [effort: L] [impact: High]

## 4. Citizen Engagement
- **Africa's Talking SMS**: SMS submission for citizen questions. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **WhatsApp chatbot**: WhatsApp interface for retrieving nominee dossiers. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Email digests**: Weekly updates on active vetting processes. [effort: S] [impact: Med]
- **Petition co-signing**: Allow citizens to cryptographically sign petitions against nominees. [effort: L] [impact: Med]
- **Shareable cards**: Auto-generated social media images for flags. [effort: M] [impact: High]

## 5. Accountability Scoring
- **Committee participation score**: Rank MPs based on attendance and questioning in vetting committees. [effort: M] [impact: Med]
- **Chapter Six compliance index**: Score nominees on a strict Chapter Six interpretation. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Vetting quality score**: Grade committees on how thoroughly they vet nominees. [effort: M] [impact: Med]
- **Citizen alignment score**: How often an MP votes in alignment with citizen submitted sentiment. [effort: M] [impact: High]

## 6. Longitudinal Tracking
- **Multi-episode tracker**: 'Has this nominee been vetted before?' showing past outcomes. [effort: S] [impact: High]
- **Rubber-stamp tracking**: Track record of MPs who consistently approve flagged nominees. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Career migration map**: Visualizing nominees jumping between ministries despite flags. [effort: M] [impact: Med]
- **Network analysis**: Visualizing connections between flagged nominees and approving MPs. [effort: L] [impact: Med]

## 7. Media & CSO Tools
- **Embeddable flag widget**: Allows news sites to embed nominee scorecards in articles. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **CSV export**: Export hearing records for data journalists. [effort: S] [impact: Med]
- **Press release generator**: Auto-generate press releases based on hearing discrepancies. [effort: M] [impact: Low]
- **API access for CSOs**: Dedicated API endpoints for civil society organizations. [effort: M] [impact: High]

## 8. Governance Tech
- **Auto-notify departments**: Webhook alerts to departments receiving flagged nominees. [effort: S] [impact: Low]
- **Parliamentary calendar sync**: Auto-sync hearing dates with parliamentary calendar API. [effort: M] [impact: Med]
- **Gazette webhook**: Trigger new episode creation on Gazette publication. [effort: M] [impact: High]
- **Blockchain record**: Anchor final vote tallies and flags on a public ledger for immutability. [effort: L] [impact: Low]
